## H84-THMS hypotheses already in the repository

The H84 lane preregistration contains five mechanisms: THM silica-seal/fault-valve indicators; 1 m LiDAR plus 10 m terrain/fluvial-chi scarp bridging; up-dip gravity/basement/magnetic coupling; hydrothermal radiometric alteration; and tip/relay stress bridging. Their descriptions, data layers, named mimics and inherited ablations are in `registry/preregistration_h84_thms.json`, `research/h84_thms_hypotheses.md`, and `evidence/h84_exp1_canary.json` / `evidence/h84_exp2_dualseed_holdout.json`.

No sixth hypothesis or fourth experiment was started: EXP-84-1 through EXP-84-3 already exhaust the declared three-experiment budget. Candidate ranking and expected gains in the older pages are priors from that work, not new measurements. A further study must start only after lane assignment and preregistration; no submission slot should be used before a full candidate-specific registry audit and selector approval.

## Limitations and next work

1. Obtain or rebuild the full registry corpus in this environment and run candidate-specific surface and final-dot checks for all 1,143 registry rasters. Do not reuse the LITI measurements as H84 evidence.
2. If the scan fails the literal >70% dot-overlap or >0.90 rank-correlation rule, record duplicate and stop that candidate. Do not waive universal blockers without an agreed upstream protocol change.
3. If it passes, independently verify the exact final TIFF hash, run the strict shared format validator, and request promotion via the separate selector within the live weekly cap.
4. The fresh audit records the official competition ID 306 and a public leaderboard snapshot; this review did not itself fetch DrivenData. The current weekly slot cap and any H84-THMS submission-page receipt remain unavailable. The public score does not clear the candidate or authorize a slot.
5. Although a static TIFF exists, no claim is made that it will beat H33 or the public leaderboard top. Such claims require comparable evaluation, and any candidate score must be recorded from an organizer submission-page receipt.
