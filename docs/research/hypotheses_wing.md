**Ranking rationale:** Rank 1 is cheapest (no large download, no external API, bridge-only), directly tests whether catalogue gaps are predictive, and is guaranteed distinct from H84-RFD's radiometric×topography. Ranks 2–4 require the 418 MB training_features split download (5 parts via gh api, verified SHA256 4371c82e..., see data_manifest.json) but are plausible multi-physics extensions with higher expected gain (magnetics sees basement). Rank 5 is most speculative (Great Basin microseismicity rate is low; see USGS NSHM) and egress is blocked for ComCat host, so marked not verifiable this session.

**Fixed design for Rank 1 (implemented):** deterministic seed 289926028; dot budget 37,820 (unique vs 37,654 of H33, 40,199 of H27-4, 44,090 of D2.8); separation 3 px; catalogue masking pixel-exact. Holdout = gems57-pooled-hide-v2, seed 20, 4 quadrants, whole chunk withholding (max_len_px 12), pooled DTI α0.2 β0.8 300 m triangular, 1000-draw spatial-cluster bootstrap. Surfaces for holdout were **not** recomputed from visible-only catalogue per fold (leakage caveat — see run-card); this blocks promotion. No tuning after holdout.
