Our operating rule

“Maximize P(Win)” and “Own the Outcome.” A score is only a score when correctly labeled; a candidate is not a submission until it passes both scientific evaluation and file-format checks. Negative results stay visible. A holdout proxy against known catalogue faults cannot establish discovery of genuinely uncatalogued faults.
